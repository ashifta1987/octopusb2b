What the app is
The app lets Octopus B2B sell turnkey projects (a full business set-up, sourced and
shipped from China) and get paid for every service before the work starts. The whole app
is in English.
Business: Octopus B2B is a China trading company. It helps clients with layout
drawing, sourcing, quality control (QC), factory inspection, shipment and handover.
Clients: mostly from Turkey, buying a complete project such as a gym, laundry,
vending or robotic cafe.
Catalogue: 300 projects, each with a photo, shown on the front page.
Main revenue: paid consultation, QC and factory inspection. Every process is charged
and paid before work starts.
Newer services: Visit China with us (guided factory and sourcing trips) and a
dedicated account manager for each project.
Price messaging: every price is shown as a starting price, because some
consultations cost more.
Supplier details: hidden from clients. The client deals only with Octopus.
Prices and fee rules
Every fee is labelled "From $X" and paid before the work starts. Staff set the exact fee per
client; a fee above 2x the starting fee needs admin approval.

The 8-step flow, payment gates and roles
Every project moves through eight phases, and each paid phase stays locked until the
client's payment is confirmed.
1. Enquiry: the client picks a project or requests a new one and fills in the plan.
2. Consultation: paid first; staff set the exact fee.
3. Design: layout drawing, 3 free versions, then 50% of the drawing fee per extra
version.
4. Sourcing: quotes from suppliers, hidden from the client.
5. Quote and deposit: the client approves the quote and pays the deposit.
6. Production and QC: factory audit and inspections, each paid before it starts.
7. Shipment: booking, tracking and shipping documents.
8. Handover: final documents, sign-off and optional China visit.
