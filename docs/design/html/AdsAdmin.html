Rules

How the money flows

1. Advertiser tops up their wallet and sets a bid and daily budget.2. Each valid click deducts the bid from their wallet (never more than the daily budget).3. The amount is booked as revenue in Finance & revenue under "Advertising (CPC)", with a monthly invoice to the advertiser.4. Invalid clicks are filtered and never charged.
