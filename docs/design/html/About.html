Who we are

A trading company first, a platform second.

Octopus B2B is a global sourcing marketplace operated by Shenzhen Octopus Trading Co. Ltd., a Chinese trading company with offices in four of the country's biggest sourcing hubs. We buy from the factory, we sell to you, and we handle the shipping end to end — one invoice, one timeline, one phone number.

Buyers see a product and a price; behind the scenes our people visit the factory, inspect the batch, print the labels and settle any claim. The platform is just the fastest way to work with us.

Merchant of recordOctopus is the seller on every order. T&Cs under Hong Kong law.
Boots on the groundTeams in Shenzhen, Foshan, Ningbo and Yiwu visit factories weekly.
Global reachShipping to 220+ countries via YunExpress and 3PL partners.
Compliance coveredCE, RoHS, FCC, FDA testing and paperwork arranged for your market.
