How a MOQ pool works

  1. Pledge your quantity and pay a 10% deposit from your wallet or by card. Your deposit is held in escrow by Octopus.
  2. Wait for the pool to fill. Share the pool link with partners if you want. New buyers' pledges push the price down for everyone.
  3. If the pool fills before the deadline: every buyer gets a proforma invoice for the balance (90%) at the pool price. Pay within 48 hours.
  4. Octopus places one order with the supplier. QC inspection is shared. Each buyer gets a separate shipment to their address with YunExpress tracking.
  5. If the pool does not fill: all deposits are refunded to each buyer's wallet. No one is charged.
