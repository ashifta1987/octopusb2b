How group buying works on Octopus

1Join or start a poolPick an open pool, pledge your quantity (minimum 100 pcs), and pay a 10% deposit from your wallet or card.
2Pool fillsAs more buyers join, the per-piece price drops. Share the pool link with partners to fill faster.
3Pay the balanceIf the pool fills before the deadline, you get a proforma invoice for the balance at the final pool price. Pay within 48 hours.
4One PO, separate shipmentsOctopus places one order with the supplier, runs a shared QC inspection, then ships each buyer separately with YunExpress tracking. If the pool does not fill, every deposit is refunded.
