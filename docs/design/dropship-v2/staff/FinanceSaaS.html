SaaS billing

Finance · decide when Octopus Dropship plan fees start, and how long the free trial is. Changes need a second approver.

New screen
  1. One switch starts or stops plan fees for everyone. While it is off, every plan is free.
  2. When it is switched on, each merchant gets a 30-day free trial first, and approves the plan in Shopify before any charge.
  3. Per-merchant overrides: free forever for Octopus group companies and test accounts, or a longer trial.

Charge plan fees

Off since launch
Off: every merchant uses their plan free. Nothing is billed in Shopify or by Octopus. Merchants see "Plans are free for now".

Proposed by one Finance user, approved by another (company-wide Finance or admin). Every change is in the audit log.

Free trial

Paid orders always keep shipping.

Shopify can restart a trial after a reinstall, so Octopus sends Shopify only the days left.

What switching on does

  1. Day 0: charging switched on
    3 merchants start a 30-day free trial; Shopify asks each to approve their plan
  2. Day 23 and day 28
    Reminder in the app (and email when app email is live)
  3. Day 30: trial ends
    Approved plans start; Shopify bills them
  4. Day 37: grace ends
    Not approved: new pushes paused, orders keep shipping
Switching off again later

Stops new charges and cancels active plan subscriptions after a confirmation. Charges already made are not refunded automatically.
