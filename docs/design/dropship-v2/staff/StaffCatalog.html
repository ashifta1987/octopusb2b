What changed on this screen
  1. An HS review queue: every code is checked by a person before the product can be published.
  2. Restricted-goods flags (battery, liquid, magnet, powder) and country of origin on every product.
  3. Each route splits the price into product, shipping, and duties and tax, with the margin shown to staff only.
  4. Products can be made visible to one merchant only, for items sourced on request.
  5. A product can't be published while any active route is missing its duty and tax.
  6. Dropship products get their own model number series (DS-000001), separate from Sourcing; the sourcing model no. is kept here for staff only.
