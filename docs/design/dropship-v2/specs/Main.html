What changes in Octopus Dropship

Same screens and journeys as the original design. Now built inside octopusb2b, paid through the system that is already live, and following Shopify's current rules.

How to use this canvas

5 pages: Overview and specs · Merchant app – desktop (24 screens) · Merchant app – mobile · States · Staff back office (10 screens). Press Play on any screen to click through, or start from the journeys.

Open the journeysComponents and specs

One platform

  • Dropship menu inside your Octopus account
  • One login, one wallet, one support desk
  • Staff work in /office/dropship
  • Request a product opens Octopus sourcing

Shopify, the current way

  • Works inside Shopify admin
  • Store access renews by itself
  • Octopus is a fulfilment location
  • Plan fees billed by Shopify

Money

  • Customers pay orders from the wallet or with payment protection
  • Pay later only for Octopus group companies and testing
  • Landed cost (product, shipping, duties) on every screen
  • Margin after duties and processing, not before
  • One Packing and shipping screen

When orders go wrong

  • Risky and bad-address orders held
  • Missed orders recovered every hour
  • Returns, lost parcels and claims
  • Re-weigh charges with photo and dispute
