Pricing and sync

Set your margin once. Cost, stock and exchange-rate changes follow your rules.

What changed
  1. Prices are set in your store's currency, with a named exchange rate and a repricing threshold.
  2. Rules never go below your minimum margin; you can approve price changes before they reach your store.
  3. Duties (DDP or DAP) moved to Packing and shipping, so they are set in one place.
