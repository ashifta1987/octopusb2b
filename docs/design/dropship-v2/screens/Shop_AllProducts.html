Changed: one page, six separate sections
  1. Products are not mixed. Each way to buy has its own coloured section in a fixed order: Sourcing, MOQ pools, Dropshipping, Warehouse (3PL), Turnkey projects, Make My Brand.
  2. Each section shows 4 items with the right buttons for that type and a 'See all' link to its own page.
  3. The jump bar under the title stays at the top while scrolling and takes the customer straight to a section. Category and Ships to apply to every section.
BUILD NOTES FOR ANTIGRAVITY
Route: /shop/all
Data: per section: top 4 by the chosen sort, filtered by category and ships-to; count per type
  • Section order is fixed. A section with nothing in the chosen category shows one line, 'No [type] in this category yet', plus See all. It is never hidden without a message.
  • 'See all' goes to that type’s own page: /shop/products, /shop/pools, /shop/dropship, /shop/warehouse, /shop/turnkey, /shop/brand.
  • Each section loads on its own; if one fails it shows 'Couldn’t load this section · Try again' and the others still show.
  • Dropshipping prices only with Dropship access; otherwise 'Request access' and no price. Closed or full pools are not listed. No supplier names.
  • Section colour always comes with its name and icon (never colour alone). Jump bar links move focus to the section heading.
  • Warehouse (3PL) cards are services, not products: rates come from the 3PL rate settings ([RATE] until the owner sets them); 'Get a quote' opens the 3PL quote form, prefilled with the service.
  • Phone: each section’s cards scroll sideways; the jump bar scrolls sideways and stays at the top.
