Plan and billing

Your plan, its limits, and every Octopus invoice and statement.

What changed on this screen
  1. For Shopify stores the plan is billed by Shopify and appears on the Shopify bill (Shopify requires this). Stores without Shopify pay the plan to Octopus.
  2. Order costs stay separate: paid from the Octopus wallet, with Octopus tax invoices and weekly statements.
  3. Before a downgrade you see exactly which products go over the limit, and you choose which ones become drafts.
  4. While Octopus has plan fees switched off, plans are free; when they start, every merchant gets a 30-day free trial first.
Plans are free for nowNEW

Octopus isn't charging plan fees yet. Before charging starts you get 30 days free, and you approve the plan in Shopify first. Order costs are paid from your wallet as usual.

Your plan is billed by ShopifyNEW

The Growth plan appears on your Shopify bill. Product, shipping and duty costs are paid to Octopus from your wallet.
