What changed on this screen
  1. Each card shows the landed cost to the selected country: product, shipping, and duties and taxes. Margin is after duties.
  2. Each country shows whether duties are included in your price (DDP) or paid by the buyer on delivery (DAP). You set this once, in Packing and shipping.
  3. Processing (pick, pack and standard packaging) is its own line on every card, so the margin shown is the real one.
  4. "Only for you" products: items Octopus sourced from your own requests, visible to you alone.
  5. Request a product opens an Octopus sourcing request, handled by the sourcing team.
  6. Each card shows the dropship model number (DS-…). Sourcing model numbers are never shown here.
