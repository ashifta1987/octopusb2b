MOQ pools

MOQ pools: the bulk price without the bulk order

Factories have minimums. Pools let several buyers share one order, and everyone pays the bulk price.

Foldable pet crateExample
Pool price US$ 11.20Alone: US$ 13.90
640 of 1,000 units · 14 buyers · closes in 5 days
  1. Pick a pool and choose your units, from 50.
  2. Pay a deposit that is held, not spent.
  3. Pool fills: the factory produces, you pay the balance after the quality check.
  4. Pool doesn’t fill: your deposit goes back to your wallet automatically.

FAQ

Questions

Can’t find your answer? Our team replies within one business day.

Who can join?

Companies only. You verify your company after sign-up; browsing and requests work right away, payments open after verification.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. You pay for what you order. Dropshipping has a small fee per order, shown before you list a product.

Can I order samples?

Yes. Most sourcing products have samples you can order from the product page.

What if a pool doesn’t fill?

Your deposit goes back to your wallet on the closing date. Nothing else is charged.

Can I keep my goods in China and ship later?

Yes. Use our Warehouse (3PL): we store, check and ship when you’re ready, in bulk or order by order.

Will my customers see your name?

No. With dropshipping and Make My Brand, your customers only see your brand.
