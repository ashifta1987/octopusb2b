What changed
  1. The app now works inside Shopify admin (Shopify requires this for App Store apps). The same screens stay at hostoctopus.com.
  2. Store access renews by itself every hour. If it can't, the merchant sees Reconnect and nothing is lost.
  3. Octopus becomes a fulfilment location in the store, so orders for Octopus products come to us automatically.
  4. Merchants see exactly what the app can and can't access, and how long buyer data is kept.

What the app can do

  • Create and edit the products you push
  • Update stock at the Octopus location
  • Read your store's orders, and act only on items supplied by Octopus
  • Add tracking and mark those items shipped

It does not ask for your customer list, discounts or themes. Other suppliers' items in an order are ignored and not stored.

Buyer dataNEW

Buyer names and addresses are used only to ship the order. They are deleted [N] days after delivery, except what invoices must keep by law.
