Packing and shippingNEW

How your orders are packed, which shipping method each Shopify rate uses, and who pays duties. One place for all of it.

New screen
  1. Processing (pick and pack) is its own line on every price: [FEE] first item, [FEE] each extra item, standard packaging included.
  2. Choose your packaging: standard, protective, or your own branded packaging stored at the Octopus warehouse.
  3. Map each Shopify shipping rate to our Economy, Standard or Express, with a fallback so no order waits.
  4. Duties (DDP or DAP) are set here per country, and nowhere else.
  5. Shipping is priced on billed weight, combined for multi-item orders; small weighing differences are absorbed by Octopus.

Extras

Weighing rule

Every parcel is weighed and measured after packing. If it is within 50 g or 10% of the estimate, Octopus absorbs the difference. Above that, you get an adjustment with a photo and 7 days to dispute it.
