BUILD NOTES FOR ANTIGRAVITY
Route: /shop/cart
Data: carts (one per company, shared by its team), cart_items (type: sample, stock, pool, brand, 3pl, quote; qty; price snapshot), price_tiers, stock, pools, rate cards
  • The part is chosen by item type: factory-made products go to Get a quote; everything with a fixed price goes to Buy now.
  • Prices are checked again when the cart opens and at checkout; changes are shown (see States).
  • Pool lines hold a deposit (30% here; the % comes from pool settings). Quantity follows the pool step.
  • The cart belongs to the company and is saved across devices and sign-ins.
  • 'Send quote request' creates one RFQ with one line per product (or one RFQ per product, grouped). The lines leave the cart.
  • Prices are examples; real prices come from price tiers, stock and the warehouse rate card.
