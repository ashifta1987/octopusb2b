## DB (live) — read supabase/migrations/20261101000110_assistant.sql and supabase/tests/assistant.test.mjs
- Customers are ORGS: account_managers(org_id PK, customer_user_id, staff_user_id, team, is_primary, assigned_at, assigned_by, reason).
- assistant_sessions(id, org_id, customer_user_id, title, channel page|drawer|api, page_context, status bot|escalated|human|closed, low_confidence_turns, escalation_id, started_at, last_message_at, ended_at)
- assistant_messages(id, session_id, org_id, author bot|customer|staff|system, author_user_id, content, confidence, tools_called jsonb, hidden_from_customer, latency_ms, tokens, created_at) — in the Realtime publication (with sessions, escalations).
- escalations(id, session_id, org_id, reason customer_request|sensitive_topic|low_confidence|negative_sentiment|needs_approval|vip|after_hours|timeout|staff_take_over, confidence, excerpt, assigned_to (null = team queue), team, ticket_id, requested_at, sla_due_at, taken_by_staff_id, taken_at, resolved_at, outcome)
- assistant_feedback(message_id, org_id, user_id, thumbs_up, note)
- profiles: + team, out_of_office, title, assistant_history_opt_out.
- Settings: assistant_enabled, assistant_confidence_threshold (0.60), assistant_sensitive_topics (jsonb list), assistant_vip_customer_ids, assistant_team_country_map {"default":"Sourcing", "<CC>": "<team>"}, assistant_max_customers_per_manager (50), assistant_escalation_sla_minutes (60), assistant_rate_limit_per_min (20), assistant_office_hours {timezone, days[1-7], start, end}.
- Permission area `assistant`: view, reply, take_over, close, reassign, configure (sourcing_agent + support: view/reply/take_over/close; operations: view; admin all). Demo: support@ is also sourcing_agent, team Sourcing, and is the buyer org's account manager.
- Views: assistant_session_mine_view (customer), assistant_queue_view (staff; RLS limits rows to customers the staff member may see).
- Customer RPCs: start_assistant_session(p_channel, p_page) → id; post_customer_message(p_session, p_content, p_page) → message id (RATE_LIMITED, SESSION_CLOSED, INVALID_MESSAGE, NOT_FOUND); assistant_feedback_set(p_message, p_thumbs_up, p_note); set_assistant_opt_out(p_opt_out); my_account_team() → {staff_user_id, name, title, team, office, out_of_office, email, phone, assigned_at, hours}; escalate_session(p_session, p_reason, p_excerpt, p_confidence) (customer may call with 'customer_request').
- Service-role RPCs: record_bot_reply(p_session, p_content, p_confidence 0..1, p_tools jsonb, p_latency_ms, p_tokens) → {message_id, low_confidence_turns, should_escalate_low_confidence}; assign_account_manager(p_org, p_reason). escalate_session also callable with service role.
- Staff RPCs: staff_assistant_reply(p_session, p_content, p_internal); take_over_session(p_session); resolve_escalation(p_session, p_outcome, p_close_session); log_assistant_open(p_session) (call on every open → audit); set_out_of_office(p_value); reassign_account_manager(p_org, p_staff, p_reason); assistant_metrics(p_from, p_to) → {sessions, resolved_by_bot, escalated, bot_resolution_rate, escalation_rate, satisfaction, sla_breaches, sla_breach_rate, avg_latency_ms, tokens}; assistant_staff_can(p_org).
- escalate_session: writes escalation, opens a Support ticket with transcript, assigns account manager (or team queue if out of office), notifications, outbox `assistant.escalated` (email/WhatsApp), and inserts the system message "Octo handed this to <name>. …". Idempotent while an escalation is open.
