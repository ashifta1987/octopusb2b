Questions

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product and the branding method. The minimum quantity is shown on each product and confirmed in your quote: [MOQ per product and method].

How long does a brand project take?

It depends on the product, the branding method, the sample and shipping. Your quote confirms the delivery date before you pay. Typical extra time for branding: [days].

Who owns the artwork files?

You do. Your logo and artwork files are kept private to your account and are used only for your orders.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. You approve an artwork proof and then a physical sample before production starts. The sample cost is shown in your quote.

How do I pay?

From your US dollar wallet in My account, which you top up by bank transfer. We do not take card payments. Your quote shows the deposit to start and the balance before shipping.
